Your planner and your choices

This policy covers Fiivi's local planner configuration. Your routines, activities, habits, preferences and progress are saved on your device. This configuration does not offer Fiivi account sign-in or Fiivi cloud synchronization. Apple handles App Store subscriptions. You choose whether to allow calendar access, notifications and Live Activities, or to share a saved file.

Fiivi does not automatically send your planner to the operator in this configuration. Fiivi has no advertising or cross-app tracking features, and does not operate a separate planner analytics or crash-upload service. Apple, the website host and services you choose may handle information under their own policies. Information you choose to email for support or include in a file may reach the operator if the message is received. The declared contact address does not guarantee message delivery.

Fiivi does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or HealthKit access. It does not receive payment-card numbers or your Apple Account password. You can ask a general support question without sending your planner or private notes.

Optional device permissions

Calendar. If you choose to connect a calendar, iOS asks for full calendar access. Fiivi reads upcoming timed events in a 14-day window and lets you choose what to import. All-day events are excluded from that list. It does not modify source calendars or continuously synchronize imported copies. Revoke access in iOS Settings to stop new access. Remove previously imported activities separately if you no longer want those copies.

Notifications. Fiivi schedules local reminders using planning details and timing preferences. It has no developer-operated remote push service. Notification text may appear on your Lock Screen or connected Apple devices. Control permissions and previews in iOS Settings.

Live Activities. When enabled and supported, routine steps, timers and progress may appear on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Fiivi updates them locally using Apple's ActivityKit and App Intents; it has no Live Activity push server. Hide routine details hides routine and step names on that surface. It does not hide notification previews, screenshots or exports. Dismissing a Live Activity does not delete the routine.

Apple services and subscriptions

Apple processes payments and maintains purchase records under Apple's privacy policy. Fiivi uses verified StoreKit information to check subscription access. Restore Purchases checks eligible subscription access; it does not recover your planner.

Apple may process App Store use, diagnostics and crash information according to its policies and your settings, and provide permitted reports to developers. Apple's independent processing is separate from Fiivi's local planner storage.

Saved files, backups and deletion

While you have app access, You → File backup lets you save a JSON file or restore a compatible Fiivi file. An export can include your planner, notes, preferences, history, streak records and local identifiers. Sharing or saving it gives the chosen destination access to its contents. An export may reach us only if you choose to send it and it is received. A temporary export can remain in app temporary storage until replaced or removed by the operating system.

Importing a compatible file replaces this device's plan after confirmation. Fiivi first keeps a recovery copy in app storage. Apple's device backups and migration may include app data depending on your settings; Fiivi does not operate or guarantee those services.

Local planning data remains until you change or delete it, reset app data or remove the app, subject to operating-system and backup behavior. Subscription expiry alone does not erase it. While you have app access, You → File backup → Delete local data resets saved planning data and removes internal before-import, before-account-restore and before-start-over recovery copies. It does not cancel a subscription or erase saved/shared exports, temporary export copies or external backups. Manage those separately through Files, device settings or your backup provider.

Main app tools, including export and local reset, require app access. If access has expired, removing the app is a separate device-level option; it does not cancel billing or remove external backups. Contact support for guidance. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a planner that has remained only on your device.

Support information

If received for support, information in the delivered message will be handled for the purposes below. The declared contact is a Gmail address; Google and your email provider may handle delivery. This policy does not guarantee delivery or a response. See Google's privacy policy for Google's processing.

We will use support information to answer your request, investigate the issue, maintain necessary service and security records, and meet applicable legal obligations. We will not sell support information or use it for unrelated marketing or targeted advertising. Send only relevant details. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

We will keep support information only while needed to handle the request, investigate a continuing issue, maintain necessary security records or meet an applicable legal obligation. These purposes determine retention; this policy does not promise an automatic deletion deadline. You may ask us to delete information you sent to support. We will review the request and explain any information that must be retained and why.

International handling and privacy requests

The operator is in the United States. If the operator receives information for support, it may be accessed in the United States. Google and GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries as described in their privacy policies.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a lawful basis, we will use support information to respond to requests concerning our service or contract, pursue legitimate interests in answering general enquiries and protecting the service, or meet legal obligations. Supplying support information is voluntary, but we may need relevant details to resolve a request. Fiivi's local planner does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of information we hold. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing; this does not undo lawful earlier processing. Email getfiivi@gmail.com. We may request proportionate information to verify the request and will explain applicable limits. For information held independently by Apple, Google or GitHub, you may also contact that provider.

You may complain to your relevant privacy regulator, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office, Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, or New Zealand Privacy Commissioner. Nothing in this policy limits rights that apply to you.

Security and younger users

Fiivi uses iOS app storage protections and Apple's transaction verification. Protect your device and take care with shared files, screenshots and Lock Screen previews. No storage or communication method is guaranteed completely secure.

Fiivi is a general-purpose planner. It does not provide a parental-consent service or claim to verify users' ages. App Store content ratings and Apple's purchase/parental controls are separate from privacy protections. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact getfiivi@gmail.com so we can review and address it under applicable law. Please avoid sending a child's planner or sensitive details unless necessary for the request.
