A little help with Fiivi

Fiivi is provided by Anthony Dale Pancho, California, United States. For app support or privacy requests, email getfiivi@gmail.com.

Something isn't working?

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and your device model, iOS/iPadOS version and Fiivi version. A screenshot may help; hide private planning details first. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information. Send a planner file only if you deliberately choose to share relevant contents for support. No particular response time is promised.

Manage a subscription

In iPhone or iPad Settings, tap your name → Subscriptions → Fiivi. You can also follow Apple's cancellation instructions or request an eligible refund from Apple. Deleting Fiivi or its planning data does not cancel a subscription. Applicable consumer rights still apply.

Restore access or your plan

Restore Purchases checks subscription access with Apple. It does not restore planning data. These instructions cover Fiivi's local planner configuration, which does not offer Fiivi account sign-in or cloud synchronization.

While you have app access, You → File backup → Save a Fiivi file lets you save a copy before switching devices. Use Restore from a saved Fiivi file to import a compatible file. Review the confirmation: importing replaces this device's plan, and Fiivi keeps a local recovery copy first. Apple's device backups and migration are separate services. A backup or successful restoration is not guaranteed.

Delete local data or ask about privacy

While you have app access, You → File backup → Delete local data resets saved planning data and internal recovery copies. Saved/shared exports, temporary export copies, external backups and subscription billing are separate. If access has expired, contact us for guidance. Removing the app does not cancel billing or erase external backups. We cannot retrieve or remotely erase a planner that remained only on your device.

Email getfiivi@gmail.com about information you sent to support or a privacy concern. See the Privacy Policy for handling and choices, and Terms of Use for subscriptions and the Apple app license.
