# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 22, 2026**

viiti ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the viiti app collects and how it is used.

## What We Collect

**viiti does not collect any personal data on our servers.** All data is stored locally on your device.

The app stores the following data on your device only:

- Habits you create (name, schedule, goal)
- Your onboarding preferences (selected distractions, focus goals)
- Blocking settings and Screen Time configuration
- Subscription status (managed by Apple)

## Screen Time & App Blocking

viiti uses Apple's Screen Time API (DeviceActivity and FamilyControls frameworks) to block selected apps. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

## Subscriptions & Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see, store, or process your payment information. For billing questions, contact Apple Support or manage your subscription in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

## Third-Party Services

viiti does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

viiti is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of viiti after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at [getviiti@gmail.com](mailto:getviiti@gmail.com).
