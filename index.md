# viiti Support

**viiti** helps you build focused habits and block distractions.

## Contact

Have a question or need help? Email us at [getviiti@gmail.com](mailto:getviiti@gmail.com).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**  
Open the Settings app on your iPhone → Apple ID → Subscriptions → viiti, then tap Cancel Subscription.

**How do I block apps during a focus session?**  
Tap **Start**, choose a habit, then turn on **Block distractions** in the session setup and choose the apps or categories. **Chill** lets you stop the session early. **Strict** keeps the block in place until the timer ends, even if you close viiti.

**My subscription isn't restoring — what do I do?**  
Open viiti and tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used when you originally subscribed.

**Can I use viiti without internet?**  
Yes. Habit tracking remains available on your device while offline. Apple or Google sign-in and cloud backup need an internet connection. If a backup request fails, your local progress stays available and you can retry in Settings.

**What is backed up when I sign in?**  
viiti can back up habit names and symbols, completion dates, focus session details, focused minutes, streak and freeze information, reflections, and quit notes to your account in Supabase. The account backup is linked to your account ID and may be associated with provider profile details such as email, name, or profile image when supplied. Reminder schedules, Screen Time selections, and StoreKit purchase records are not included in the habit backup. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.

**How do I delete my account and data?**  
If you are signed in, open **Settings → Delete account** while signed in to the account that owns the backup. The app clears the account's local habit data only after the server confirms deletion. If the app cannot confirm the request, it shows an error and keeps local data; treat the request as incomplete and contact [getviiti@gmail.com](mailto:getviiti@gmail.com) for help. If local cleanup fails after server confirmation, the app reports that and offers a retry. For a guest with no cloud backup, **Settings → Delete account** clears the data on that device.

Signing out keeps local habit data and does not delete the account or cloud backup. Uninstalling viiti also does not delete a cloud backup. Deleting a viiti account does not cancel an App Store subscription.
