## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**  
Open the Settings app on your iPhone > Apple ID > Subscriptions > viiti, then tap Cancel Subscription.

**How do I remove app blocking?**  
Open viiti, go to Settings, and turn off blocking. If Strict Mode is enabled, you will need to disable Screen Time in iPhone Settings to override it.

**My subscription isn't restoring — what do I do?**  
Open viiti and tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used when you originally subscribed.

**Does viiti work without an internet connection?**  
Yes. All your habits and data are stored locally on your device. No account or internet connection is required to use viiti.

**How do I delete my data?**  
Deleting viiti from your iPhone removes all locally stored data (habits, settings, preferences). There is no server-side data to request deletion of.
